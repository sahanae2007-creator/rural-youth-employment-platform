 **GRAMAJOBS AI -An Agentic AI-Powered Rural Employment and skill development platform**


**Project Overview**:

The AI-Powered Rural Youth Employment Platform aims to bridge the gap between rural youth and employment opportunities using Artificial Intelligence.

**Problem Statement**:

Rural youth cannot find work that fits , and small employers cannot find them.

**Proposed Solution**:

We propose an AI-powered platform that analyzes users' educational qualifications, skills and interests to recommend suitable job opportunities and personalized skill development programs.

**Key Features**:

- AI-based job recommendations
- Skill gap analysis
- Personalized learning recommendations
- Local employment opportunities
- Regional language accessibility
- Employer and candidate connectivity

**Proposed Technology**:

- Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- Backend: Node.js
- Database: MongoDB
- AI: Skill matching and recommendation system

**Expected Impact**:

- Improve employment accessibility for rural youth.
- Encourage skill development.
- Connect rural talent with employers.
- Promote digital inclusion.

**Project Status**:
Idea and planning stage. Prototype development is yet to begin.

**Vision**:
To ensure that geographical location does not become a barrier to employment opportunities.
